Accepted to the Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP 2026). Spotlight at the NonAR-LM Workshop at COLM 2026.

Plan-Verify-Fill builds a hierarchical skeleton of high-leverage semantic anchors and verifies it before filling, reducing the number of function evaluations by up to 65% relative to confidence-based parallel decoding without compromising accuracy.
