This preprint measures the self-consistency of cardinal preference judgments elicited from LLMs, such as stated willingness-to-pay.

Statistical tests and interpretable measures applied to flight, apartment, and hotel examples across six LLMs reveal large, persistent departures from any single self-consistent utility function.
